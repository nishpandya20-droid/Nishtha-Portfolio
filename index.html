ASTER Café & Coffee

"Bloom with every sip — A little moment of calm"

ASTER is a warm, botanical café inspired by the beauty of flowers and the comfort of slow coffee moments. The brand combines natural elements, elegant typography, and a cozy atmosphere to create a place where every sip feels like a little moment of calm.

  • ✓ Target Audience: Coffee lovers, students, remote creatives & young professionals.
  • ✓ Brand Vibe: Botanical, nature-inspired, elegant, warm & cozy.
  • ✓ Symbolism: Inspired by the Aster flower & morning star — growth, beauty, and renewal.
